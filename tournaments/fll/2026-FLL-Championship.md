# Adventist Robotics Championship Tournament

On this page you can see the results of the Adventist Robotics Championship Tournament held on May 3, 2026 at Forest Lake Academy. There are links to the start of each match in the livestream video. This is great for sharing with friends and family who weren't able to attend the tournament, so they can see the excitement and hard work on display.
