### Robot overview

Our robot is based on the GoBilda Robot in 3 Days design.  It has an intake and dual launchers with a diverter.  Our shot accuracy varies but may be around 66% overall.  We can shoot from the near or the far launch zone, but will probably focus on the near zone as we are more accurate there.

### Parking

Our robot barely fits in the parking zone.  If your robot is smaller we may want to have you go for a full park and we will go for a partial park.

We do have an auto-park which uses Pedro Pathing to go to the full park position, which helps us get into position more quickly.  However, by the end of the match our odometry has probably drifted a bit that we will need to adjust a bit after the auto-park.

If we do decide that our robot should go for full park, you would probably want to move your robot to near the secret tunnel, loading zone, or front of the field when there are 5 to 10 seconds left in the match, and then move in for a partial park once we are in position for the full park.
