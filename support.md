## Volunteer Opportunities

### Assistant Coach

You can be an assistant coach — and **you don't need to know anything about robotics or engineering**. Assistant coaches help with supervision, logistics, and encouraging students. Your presence makes a huge difference!

### Mentor

A mentor is someone with expertise in a specific area who can help our teams. That area could be something engineering-related like:

- **Programming** (Python, Java, or just general coding concepts)
- **Computer-Aided Design (CAD)**
- **Custom fabrication** (3D printing, machining, etc.)

But there are also many non-technical areas where mentors could help:

- **Project management**
- **Fundraising**
- **Marketing & social media**
- **Brainstorming & creative problem solving**

If you're interested in volunteering, [contact us](/contact)!

## Join a Team

### FIRST LEGO League (FLL)
- **Grades:** 4th–8th
- **What you do:** Build and program autonomous LEGO robots, develop an innovation project, and learn core values like teamwork and discovery.

### FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC)
- **Grades:** 7th–12th
- **What you do:** Design, build, and program robots using real engineering tools and materials to compete in head-to-head matches.

Interested in joining? [Contact us](/contact) to learn more!
