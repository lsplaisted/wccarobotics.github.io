# FLL Teams — FIRST LEGO League

WCCA Robotics has two FLL teams competing in the 2025–26 **Unearthed** season (archaeology theme). FIRST LEGO League is a robotics competition for students ages 9–14 where teams build autonomous robots using LEGO SPIKE Prime, develop an innovation project, and demonstrate core values like teamwork and discovery.

---
