Overview

Each 
FIRST
 Tech Challenge competition is played in a
head-to-head tournament format. Each tournament may consist of Qualification
MATCHES and Playoff MATCHES.

Qualification MATCHES allow each team to earn MATCH points
and RANKING POINTS which determine their seeding position and may qualify them
for participation in the Playoff MATCHES.

Playoff MATCHES determine the event winning ALLIANCE.
